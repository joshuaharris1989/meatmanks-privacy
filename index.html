Privacy Policy

MeatManKs publishes You Son of a Butcher for Android (package com.yousonofabutcher.game).

Effective November 13, 2026.

What the game uses

The game works offline. It saves your settings, progress, scores, shift history, unlocks and suspended shift in app-private storage on your device. These files let the game remember your choices and recover your progress. MeatManKs does not receive or share these files through the game.

The game has no sign-in, player accounts, ads, in-app purchases, tracking, analytics or automatic crash-reporting service. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone, or use advertising identifiers.

Debug test builds can record the device model, Android version and performance measurements in a local diagnostic file when a tester explicitly enables the benchmark. The game does not upload that file.

Storage and deletion

Android restricts access to the game's local files. The game keeps save and recovery files as needed to preserve progress. To remove all game data stored by the app on your device, open Android Settings, find the game under Apps and choose Storage, then Clear storage (sometimes called Clear data). Uninstalling the game also removes its local data. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app. On some devices, Android may still transfer app data directly to a new device.

Google Play and this website

Google Play handles distribution. If you opt in to Android usage and diagnostics, Google may collect crash and performance information and make diagnostics available to MeatManKs through Play Console to help fix issues. Google's handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
